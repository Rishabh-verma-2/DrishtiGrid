SLIDE 5: VEHICLE-FIRST ANPR PIPELINE — "MODEL-BY-MODEL BREAKDOWN"
================================================================================

TITLE: Enterprise ANPR: Vehicle-First Computer Vision Pipeline

Unlike legacy ANPR systems that scan full frames blindly (catching signboards and posters),
DrishtiGrid uses a strict 8-stage Vehicle-First architecture:

1. STAGE 1 — VEHICLE DETECTION & CLASSIFICATION
   • MODEL / TECH: YOLOv8 COCO (`yolov8n.pt`)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Localizes all vehicles in the camera frame first (Cars, Motorcycles,
       Buses, Trucks, and Auto-Rickshaws).
     - Computes vehicle bounding box, class confidence, and spatial centroid.
     - Performs K-Means / HSV dominant color classification on the vehicle body.

2. STAGE 2 — VEHICLE ROI EXPANSION & SUB-SCANNING
   • MODEL / TECH: Geometric Region of Interest (ROI) Expander & Bumper Slicing
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Expands the vehicle bounding box by +8% to +15% to capture low-mounted plates.
     - Runs specialized bumper & grille sub-scans for high-chassis buses and trucks.
     - Filters out 100% of roadside billboards, highway signboards, and shop signs.

3. STAGE 3 — SPECIALIZED LICENSE PLATE LOCALIZATION
   • MODEL / TECH: Custom-Trained YOLOv8 License Plate Detector (`license_plate_detector.pt`)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Detects the exact rectangular boundary of the license plate strictly
       inside each vehicle's isolated ROI.
     - Employs multi-scale search and contour-based fallback if plates are angled.

4. STAGE 4 — ANTI-HALLUCINATION QUALITY GATE
   • MODEL / TECH: Laplacian Variance + Sobel Edge Density + Sub-pixel Resolution Checks
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Evaluates whether plate crop is readable BEFORE passing it to OCR.
     - If blurry, occluded, or under 18x6 pixels, flags as `PLATE_DETECTED_OCR_UNREADABLE`.
     - Completely eliminates OCR hallucinations (generating fake alphanumeric numbers).

5. STAGE 5 — NEURAL ILLUMINATION & CONTRAST ENHANCEMENT
   • MODEL / TECH: Zero-DCE (Zero-Reference Deep Curve Estimation, `zero_dce.pth`) + CLAHE
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Zero-DCE: Dynamically enhances pitch-dark night CCTV frames using non-linear
       light curves without introducing color artifacts.
     - CLAHE: Balances high-beam headlight glare and deep shadow contrasts.

6. STAGE 6 — DEEP LEARNING SUPER-RESOLUTION
   • MODEL / TECH: Real-ESRGAN x4plus (Residual-in-Residual Dense Block Network - RRDBNet)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Reconstructs high-frequency edge details and sharpens alphanumeric characters
       on low-resolution, distant CCTV vehicle crops (4x upscaling).

7. STAGE 7 — MULTI-VARIANT OCR ENSEMBLE VOTING
   • MODEL / TECH: PaddleOCR (DBNet for text detection + SVTR for text recognition)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Runs OCR across 5 visual variations simultaneously (Original, Grayscale,
       CLAHE, Adaptive Threshold, and Super-Resolution).
     - Takes a weighted consensus vote across character predictions for maximum accuracy.

8. STAGE 8 — BIPARTITE VEHICLE-TO-PLATE ASSOCIATION
   • MODEL / TECH: SciPy Hungarian Algorithm (`scipy.optimize.linear_sum_assignment`)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Solves global maximum plausibility matching across multiple vehicles.
     - Factors: Spatial containment, vertical bumper position, area ratio, and ROI lock.
     - Guarantees zero cross-vehicle misassignments in dense, overlapping traffic jams.

SLIDE 6: INDIAN NUMBER PLATE VALIDATION & CANONICAL REPAIR
================================================================================

TITLE: Specialized Indian License Plate Grammar & Disambiguation Engine

• INDIAN REGULATORY PARSER
  - Implements Positional Grammar Validation across all Indian vehicle registration types.
  - Recognizes 4 distinct plate categories:
    1. Standard Private Plates: [State 2L][District 2D][Series 1-3L][Number 4D] (e.g. GJ01AB1234)
    2. BH (Bharat Series) Plates: [Year 2D] BH [Number 4D] [Series 2L] (e.g. 22BH1234AA)
    3. EV Green Plates: Standard format with green background and 'E' suffix (e.g. GJ01AB1234E)
    4. Commercial & Transport Plates: Yellow/Black format with specialized series rules.

• DETERMINISTIC POSITIONAL CHARACTER DISAMBIGUATION
  - Solves common optical font confusions based on character position:
    * Positions 0–1 (State Code): Must be Alphabetic.
      - Automatically fixes: '0' -> 'O', '1' -> 'I', '8' -> 'B', '5' -> 'S'
      - Validates against all 36 Indian States and Union Territories (GJ, DL, MH, RJ, KA, etc.)
    * Positions 2–3 (District Code): Must be Numeric Digits.
      - Automatically fixes: 'O'/'Q'/'D' -> '0', 'I'/'L' -> '1', 'Z' -> '2', 'B' -> '8'
    * Tail Positions (Vehicle Registration Sequence): Must be Numeric Digits.
      - Automatically repairs OCR confusion before database matching.

• FORENSIC PRESENTATION & CONFIDENCE METRICS
  - Provides dual confidence scores: Detector Confidence (%) and OCR Confidence (%).
  - Renders authentic high-contrast Indian plate pill badges with blue 'IND' visual accents.

SLIDE 7: CROWD DENSITY & PUBLIC SAFETY AI PIPELINE
================================================================================

TITLE: Crowd Monitoring, Density Estimation & Surge Detection

1. MULTI-SCALE SLICED SLIDING-WINDOW TILING (`crowd_tiling.py`)
   • TECH: Overlapping Tile Division (20%–35% overlap)
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Eliminates small-object detection drop-off in wide-angle, high-resolution CCTV.
     - Slices 4K/1080p frames into overlapping windows, runs batch YOLO inference,
       and maps detections back to global frame coordinates.

2. DEDICATED HEAD DETECTION PASS & OCCLUSION RESOLUTION
   • TECH: Secondary High-Recall Head Feature Detector
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Detects people in packed stampede conditions where torsos are 100% occluded
       and only heads/faces are visible.

3. BIPARTITE HUNGARIAN HEAD-TO-BODY PAIRING (`crowd_postprocess.py`)
   • TECH: SciPy Linear Sum Assignment Optimization
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Associates detected heads with detected pedestrian bodies based on vertical/
       horizontal alignment and bounding box overlap.
     - Suppresses duplicate head counts while promoting unassociated heads into
       `OCCLUDED_PERSON` count, giving true crowd size without double counting.

4. PLUGGABLE DENSITY ESTIMATION LAYER (`crowd_density.py`)
   • TECH: Deep Density Estimation (CSRNet / DM-Count) + Spatial Texture-Gradient Fallback
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - When individual head/body boundaries disappear into continuous crowds (e.g. rallies,
       festivals, religious gatherings), switches to neural density map integration.

5. SPATIAL DENSITY GRID & REAL-TIME SURGE ALERTS
   • TECH: Configurable Spatial Matrix (e.g. 3x4 or 4x4 Grid) + Rolling Temporal Baseline
   • WHAT WE USE IT FOR:
     - Maps crowd concentrations into tactical geographic zones (Zone A1, B2, etc.).
     - Classifies crowd threat: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL.
     - Triggers automated alerts when instantaneous headcount surges >150% above the
       camera's rolling 10-minute historical baseline.
